Featured Keynote

Dr. Hannah Fry

Mathematician, Broadcaster & Professor for the Public Understanding of Mathematics · University of Cambridge

Dr. Hannah Fry is a mathematician, broadcaster and professor for the Public Understanding of Mathematics at University of Cambridge, known for bringing complex ideas about technology, human behaviour and mathematics to life with wit, warmth and clarity. Her work includes the Emmy Award-winning The Future with Hannah Fry, BBC’s AI Confidential with Hannah Fry and The Secret Genius of Modern Life, as well as the bestselling book Hello World: How to Be Human in the Age of the Machine.

Session Highlight

At Horizons, Hannah will explore the human side of our rapidly changing relationship with AI and technology, bringing her distinctive perspective on how new systems are shaping the way we think, make decisions and work.
